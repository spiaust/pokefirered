# Expansion walkthrough

## Character customization

If WORLD OPTIONS is missing, read a modern town travel board from (19,12), facing UP (for example, in Oxford). Finish its pages and close the map to receive the item normally. Then open BAG, KEY ITEMS, WORLD OPTIONS and choose USE. UP/DOWN scrolls through every row; LEFT/RIGHT or A changes the selected preference. After FLOWER MOTION are SKIN and ACCENT. Each has five choices: CLASSIC preserves the original appearance; SKIN also offers LIGHT, TAN, BROWN and DEEP, and ACCENT offers BLUE, GREEN, PURPLE and GOLD. Accent affects backpack/belt trim according to the avatar.

Combine skin and accent choices with the existing five outfits and Original/Red/Leaf avatar selection. The live preview shows the same colors used while walking or riding the bicycle. L/R rotates the preview; SELECT switches WALK/BIKE. Save normally to retain your choices. RESTORE DEFAULTS resets every cosmetic preference after confirmation; B or START cancels.

## Lapras riding art

After earning Conrad's THUNDERBADGE, speak to the rental attendant from (26,16), facing DOWN, in London or Oxford. Accept the free rental. Lapras now has its own visible blue body, pale shell, horn and flippers, with directional frames. Steer with the D-pad; ride onto a clear bank to dismount. Normal Save/Continue works on the water. No party slot or HM is required. The existing bicycle, riverboat, ferry and rail connections remain available.

## Celebi: Letters for Tomorrow

1. Complete the main journey through Rose's London reunion and Ada's MAIN STORY COMPLETE conclusion in Oxford. With an older completed save, speak to Ada once so this build records that you have read the conclusion.
2. In Oxford, stand at (38,10), face UP and press A. Choose YES to enter the RADCLIFFE visitor room. Walk to (6,4), face UP and speak to the central archive researcher. Choose YES to start LETTERS FOR TOMORROW. Answer YES to keeping the letter's date and author together. This records the Oxford sources note.
3. Take the train to Chantilly. Stand at (51,11), face UP, press A and choose YES to enter the CHATEAU visitor room. Speak to the central researcher from (6,4), facing UP. Answer YES to labeling original material and restoration. This records the Chantilly context note.
4. Take the train to Oranienburg. Stand at (48,9), face UP, press A and choose YES to enter the PALACE visitor room. Speak to its central researcher from (6,4), facing UP. Answer YES to asking permission before publicly naming a witness. This records the Oranienburg care note. Steps 3 and 4 may be completed in either order.
5. Leave each room through its south doorway, then return by train to Oxford. Stand at (18,14), face DOWN and speak to Ada. With all three notes, she concludes LETTERS FOR TOMORROW, Celebi appears, and you receive one PP UP. Make room in BAG, ITEMS if Ada asks; your notes remain saved until you return.
6. Save normally. The chapter, notes and completion persist through Continue. Repeated conversations retain the records and do not repeat the reward. Ada's original conclusion and free exploration remain available after the chapter.

NO or B declines an offer/question without recording a note; speak again to retry. Save after any note to pause safely. This optional chapter does not gate the finished main story.

## European Council: complete championship walkthrough

After Ada says **MAIN STORY COMPLETE!**, return to London. For older completed saves, reread Ada's conclusion first. The Council is optional and preserves your main ending and archive chapter.

1. At London's reading-room door (55,33), face UP, press A and choose YES. Inside, approach the receptionist from (5,4), face UP and choose YES to enter the Council Hall.
2. Prepare a varied team around levels 22-25 or higher. The nurse at (2,7), facing UP, restores HP, status and PP freely. Coach Ivo at (6,7), facing UP, offers repeatable practice with normal experience and money. The shop at (4,7), facing UP, sells Super Potions and status medicine.
3. For a flying partner, catch Pidgey in London's northern countryside. Collect the London, Paris and Berlin guides' three stamps to earn EXP. SHARE; give it to Pidgey and train through Coach Ivo. Pidgey evolves at level 18. A flying partner helps against the Garden Regent and Rowan's Ivysaur; switch away from flying partners against electric opponents. A water/ground starter can handle Pikachu but must avoid grass attacks.
4. Challenge the north row from left to right, facing UP from (2,4), (4,4), (6,4), (8,4), then (10,4): Alfred (18-19), Solene (19-20), Otmar (20-21), Elara (21-22), Champion Rowan (22-23). Heal between matches, use BAG and POKEMON during battles, and Save normally after victories.
5. Winning the fifth match displays **EUROPEAN COUNCIL CHAMPION!** and saves your permanent title. The first championship gives one Rare Candy. If your Bag is full, free a slot and speak to the steward at (10,7), facing UP, to claim it; another battle is unnecessary.
6. Leave through either south door to reception, then its south door to London. Victories remain saved when you leave or lose. Defeat returns you to London's clinic with normal money loss; return to retry the current match. After finishing, the steward offers a new circuit. YES resets match progress while retaining your title; repeat championships do not award another Rare Candy.

See [European Council guide](EUROPEAN-COUNCIL.md) for each team, preparation and saved-state details. USA companion transfer is deferred until that game exists and its engine/save protocol can be integrated.
