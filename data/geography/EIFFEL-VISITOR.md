# Eiffel visitor room (v0.69)

This fictional compressed visitor room uses native furnishings. It is not
an architectural reconstruction of a real Eiffel Tower interior. A guide
and two exhibits describe observing the garden and river with care.
Dialogue is repeatable, with no rewards or new story flags.

Face the tower's southern base at (9,36) from (9,37) and press A. Both
exit tiles return to (9,37). The room is appended after all existing maps.
The entire Paris outdoor map is identical to the v0.68 fixture, including
collision, elevations and old saved positions. Notre-Dame is unaffected.

Regenerate with scripts/build-eiffel-visitor.py and then the Paris capital
map. Verify with scripts/test_eiffel_static.py, scripts/test_eiffel_visitor.py,
scripts/test_capital_realism.py Paris and the Notre-Dame case test.
