# Playable landmark cases (v0.60)

Three modern landmarks now have compact, explorable visitor areas. These
are fictional Pokemon spaces inspired by their locations, not accurate
architectural interiors or accounts of real events.

| City | Enter from | Investigation | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paris | Face north at (28,35), press A | Notre-Dame: The Quiet Bells | Spell Tag |
| London | Face north at (14,35), press A | Westminster: A Place to Rest | Rare Candy |
| Berlin | Face north at (21,33), press A | Reichstag: The Broken Signal | Three Great Balls |

Speak to the curator, inspect both records in either order, then approach
the disturbance at the back. Each resolution is peaceful. Return to the
curator for the reward. A full Bag preserves the pending reward. Cases
save independently; the field ledger summarizes them. Step onto the red
floor marker at the south exit to return outside.

After calming Notre-Dame's Gastly, the same encounter point offers a
repeatable, optional level-12 Gastly battle. It can be caught normally.
Capturing it is not required to resolve the case.

## Story direction

Oak studies how Pokemon share cities, forests and old roads. The regional
studies establish the player's field skills; gyms and the rival remain the
training thread. The three landmark cases reveal disturbed shelter and
routes: building work, a blocked roost and a faulty temporary signal.
Their records connect to earlier efforts to keep companions together.

After the existing badge prerequisite, Ada sends the player to investigate
Chantilly's stopped watches. Celebi opens the historical journey. The
player follows a chain of care from Chantilly through northern France,
Le Havre and Southampton to London's reception. Local tasks concern
shelter, communication, reunions and practical help. Rose provides the
arrival conclusion; Ada connects it back to present-day fieldwork.

The Le Havre ferry now requires the captain's dock clearance. London's
transport requires the returned luggage. Neither leg requires a detour
to Oxford. Optional reports remain available, including after arrival in
London, and preserve their journal records and return-travel unlocks.
Earlier chapter prerequisites remain unchanged.

Landmark cases are optional and can be played in any order. They do not
retroactively gate existing saves or badge progress. Three completed cases
unlock a shared interpretation in the ledger and Ada's conclusion. Other
landmarks remain exteriors for now.

## Visual treatment and limits

Outdoor trees use complete native silhouettes, with compact trees and
bushes for narrow strips. Collision and elevations are retained. Source
landmark art is filtered before reduction to the GBA palette, reducing
jagged detail. The final game still uses a 240x160 pixel display and limited
tile palettes; these changes do not turn it into a high-resolution game.

Map groups append three interiors without shifting existing map indices.
Case variables 0x40C0 through 0x40C2 were unused in this project. No save
structure changes are required. Use normal battery-save Continue after
updating the ROM; emulator save states contain the old running program.
