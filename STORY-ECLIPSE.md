# Pokemon Europe: Eclipse — story bible

## Premise and cast

The European League links trainers through eight national gyms. ADA studies
how partners recover from fear. The named rival starts ambitious and dismissive
of help; a failed solo rescue in Prague changes that outlook. Director VEIL leads
Team ECLIPSE, a modern criminal organization that sells forced obedience as
strength. CELEBI follows damage caused by a stolen temporal clock.

MIREILLE, ZOFIA and LENA are fictional French, Polish and German resistance
contacts. The WWII antagonists are Nazi occupation patrols and a fictional
Nazi research operation. The player always helps the resistance. A Germany
start means local knowledge and a repair-courier cover in Berlin, not allegiance
to Nazism. All three starts reach the same moral choices and ending.

The historical chapter is explicitly alternate-fiction inside real wartime
contexts. Pokemon experiments are invented. The player rescues victims of
that operation; defeating a trainer does not undo the Holocaust or win WWII.
No real persecuted community is replaced with Pokemon. No Nazi insignia is
used as a reward or player customization. Dark themes center coercion, loss,
recovery and responsibility; rescued partners survive and can heal.

## Campaign, beginning to ending

### 1. Oxford — England (level 6)

ADA: Welcome to the European League. Your rival believes strength means never needing help. Show them what a partner can do. The station porter will open the next journey after your match.

Consequence: {RIVAL}: You won. A League courier vanished near Paris. I am going after the badges, but I will keep an eye out. First, earn your badge here.

### 2. London — England (level 12)

ELLIS: Eight countries host our League challenge. Rock is your first test. Team ECLIPSE calls frightened POKEMON powerful. We call that cruelty.

Consequence: ELLIS: The BOULDERBADGE is yours. Take the ferry to France. Find ADA's missing courier before ECLIPSE does.

### 3. Paris — France (level 17)

MARINE: The courier left a sealed research ledger. ECLIPSE stole its key and abandoned a PIKACHU whose heart is closed. Win this match, then meet our rescue keeper.

Consequence: MARINE: The CASCADEBADGE is yours. The ledger names Brussels. Take the rescued partner with you if you can. Care opens what force cannot.

### 4. Brussels — Belgium (level 21)

SABINE: ECLIPSE buys League freight permits to move stolen partners. Our garden shelter intercepted one shipment. Prove your bond before I entrust you with its manifest.

Consequence: SABINE: You earned the RAINBOWBADGE. The manifest leads to Amsterdam. Their director VEIL funds the machines through a shipping company.

### 5. Amsterdam — Netherlands (level 25)

MAARTEN: VEIL's machines use a signal that suppresses emotion. Your rival chased a freighter without waiting for us. Earn the THUNDERBADGE, then find them in Berlin.

Consequence: MAARTEN: The THUNDERBADGE is yours. ECLIPSE stole an archive from Berlin. ADA thinks the signal is much older than this company.

### 6. Berlin — Germany (level 29)

GRETA: Our archive preserves the work of people who resisted the Nazi regime. Someone stole a wartime laboratory file. German resistance members risked everything to protect their neighbors. Help us protect that memory.

Consequence: GRETA: The SOULBADGE is yours. Your birthplace never decides your loyalties. ADA traced the stolen file to Prague. The resistance names in it must remain protected.

### 7. Prague — Czechia (level 32)

ADA: ECLIPSE is testing a shadow transmitter in the old freight yard. Your rival tried to destroy it alone and was captured. Defeat the handler and bring everyone out together.

Consequence: {RIVAL}: I thought asking for help meant losing. You came anyway. The receiver points to Vienna. From now on we do this together.

### 8. Vienna — Austria (level 35)

IRENA: The signal can be unmade, but its original frequency is missing. My psychic partners hear a frightened voice inside it. Earn the MARSHBADGE; then we can search the Swiss archive.

Consequence: IRENA: The MARSHBADGE is yours. The archive's keeper in Zurich has a clock that reacts to CELEBI. ECLIPSE is trying to use it to reach the past.

### 9. Zurich — Switzerland (level 37)

VEIL: Fear creates obedient partners. CELEBI will take me to the original machine, and this time nobody will stop its broadcast. Your sentimental League is already obsolete.

Consequence: ADA: VEIL escaped with the clock's core. We recovered its map. It names Rome, Madrid and a final shipping point in Lisbon. CELEBI is following the damage to time.

### 10. Rome — Italy (level 40)

FLAVIA: ECLIPSE recruits trainers by promising instant strength. My partners grew through years of trust. The VOLCANOBADGE belongs to someone who understands that difference.

Consequence: FLAVIA: The VOLCANOBADGE is yours. A League ranger intercepted VEIL's message to Madrid. They plan to erase the people who stopped the original experiments.

### 11. Madrid — Spain (level 43)

INES: This is your eighth challenge. The EARTHBADGE will qualify you for Athens, but first there are lives to protect. ECLIPSE's ship is leaving Lisbon with the clock core.

Consequence: INES: All eight badges! The League can wait. Your rival has found a harbor path in Lisbon. Stop the ship before VEIL can reopen the past.

### 12. Lisbon — Portugal (level 45)

{RIVAL}: We will cut the harbor power together. You draw the handler out; I will stop the transmitter. CELEBI is here. Something on that ship is tearing a hole in time.

Consequence: ADA: CELEBI opened a path to 1940. We can stop VEIL's fictional shadow experiment and protect its victims. We cannot erase the real war. CELEBI will always offer a way home.

### 13. Paris1940 — France, 1940 (level 46)

MIREILLE: Nazi occupation forces seized our partners for a secret experiment. We are the resistance. Drive off this patrol so our courier can reach a safe house. CELEBI waits beside the return guide.

Consequence: MIREILLE: The courier is safe. Her message names a transfer to Warsaw in 1943. CELEBI can follow the clock's echoes. Our struggle is for people and their partners, never for a flag.

### 14. Warsaw1943 — Poland, 1943 (level 48)

ZOFIA: The underground is moving families away from a fictional research depot. Disable its shadow controller while we open the exit. The real occupation and persecution were human crimes. Our rescue changes this invented operation only.

Consequence: ZOFIA: Everyone in the safe house has a route out. The machine's designer moved to Berlin. LENA, a German resistance courier, can get you into its service wing in 1944.

### 15. Berlin1944 — Germany, 1944 (level 50)

LENA: I work inside the laboratory for the resistance. Your cover is a repair courier. If you started in Germany, your local knowledge helps us verify the papers. Once inside, refuse their orders and disable the machine. I will evacuate the partners.

Consequence: LENA: The controller is destroyed. VEIL stole a copy, but we kept the names of the resistance out of his hands. We helped this rescue succeed. The war still has its real history. Go home and stop him.

### 16. Geneva — Switzerland (level 52)

VEIL: I brought the shadow frequency back. One broadcast will close every heart in the League. Your rival has shut the outer relays, but you will not reach the core.

Consequence: ADA: It is over. The broadcast is broken; the rescued partners can heal. The archive will preserve the resistance accounts with care. Your rival is waiting in Athens. Finish the journey you began.

### 17. Athens — Greece (level 54)

THALIA: Welcome to the European League. Eight badges and the freedom of your partners brought you here. Four council members stand between you and the final rival match. Begin with ice.

Consequence: THALIA: First council match complete. Heal and rest if you need to. NIKOS is waiting at the next chamber.

### 18. Athens2 — Greece (level 55)

NIKOS: Strength is what you choose to protect. My fighting team will test how you turn that choice into action.

Consequence: NIKOS: You passed. ELENI studies the fears that remain after a battle. She is your next opponent.

### 19. Athens3 — Greece (level 56)

ELENI: Darkness is not a fault in a partner. A closed heart is an injury. Our ghost and psychic teams have learned to trust again. Show me your team's courage.

Consequence: ELENI: Your partners know you will stay with them. LEON waits in the dragon chamber.

### 20. Athens4 — Greece (level 57)

LEON: You have crossed countries and centuries. One last council match. Do not mistake a powerful partner for a tool.

Consequence: LEON: The council recognizes you. Your rival reached the champion's chamber first. Go meet them as equals.

### 21. Champion — Greece (level 60)

{RIVAL}: I used to think a champion stood alone. Now I know who helped me get here. No secrets, no stolen strength. One honest match between us. Ready?

Consequence: {RIVAL}: You are the EUROPEAN CHAMPION! ADA records our journey: eight badges, partners rescued, a resistance network protected, and ECLIPSE defeated. CELEBI returns to its forest. The shelters remain open. We will keep traveling together. THE END.

## The continental rescue network

The station network remains available after the League. Each destination has
modern local trainer challenges and a historical refuge operation. Celebi
unlocks after Lisbon's anchor chapter. Past colors become sepia; returning
restores the modern palette. Defeating the historical opponent allows a single
local decision: preserve a garden or establish a research archive. Both protect
the refuge. The future retains different scenery, dialogue and encounters,
and opens an eastern shortcut. Thirty-nine saved memories complete Ada's
rescue-network epilogue and award one Master Ball.

The wartime chapter is historical fiction. German resistance members can
work under cover against the Nazi organization. Neutral destinations use
friendly keeper trials; Nazi infiltrators elsewhere are fictional operations,
not claims that Moscow, London or every other city was occupied. Contemporary
navigation labels include regions that belonged to the USSR and Yugoslavia
during WWII. The victory does not erase the victims or real history.

## Legendary guardians

Guardians appear as native overworld Pokemon in the present-day country districts.
Their landmark signs describe the location and unlock condition. These are real
wild battles with normal catch rates and moves. Declining, fleeing or knocking
out a guardian leaves it available for another attempt; a successful catch
removes it permanently through a saved flag. Full-party catches go to the PC.
Optional Nuzlocke still uses that country's first-encounter allowance, shared
with its historical era. Repeat attempts can be caught after disabling Nuzlocke.
Celebi remains the time-travel guide so catching a guardian cannot break travel.

## Scandinavian remnant hunt

After the League ending, meet Ada northeast of Copenhagen's landmark. Accept
her investigation, defeat Vex in Copenhagen, Rime in Oslo and Boreal in Stockholm,
then return to Ada. Their three relays threaten the northern rescue network and
its guardians. Opponents have three, three and four shadow partners at Normal
levels 62, 64 and 66, with curated four-move sets, held items and stronger AI.
Easy lowers levels by four; Hard raises them by five without replacing movesets.

The hunt saves each victory independently and leaves the main ending complete.
Declining does not advance it. Defeated commanders cannot be farmed. Ada gives
one Shadow Ball TM; a full TM stack leaves it pending until there is room.
Completed saves retain the hunt, reward and open League/travel facilities.
