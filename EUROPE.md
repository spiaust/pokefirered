# Pokemon European Tour

A FireRed-based prototype of a journey through real European countries.


## Paris garden and riverside paths (v0.61)

Walk south from Paris's original square to the Eiffel Tower. Paved side
paths now circle the Champ de Mars flower beds, and the left-bank
promenade continues east toward Notre-Dame's island bridge. The Eiffel
and Seine signs give directions. Notre-Dame's entrance and investigation
work as before. Continue normally with your existing battery save.

## Landmark investigations and visual fixes (v0.60)

In modern Paris, London and Berlin, approach the south-facing entrance of
Notre-Dame, Westminster or the Reichstag, face the building and press A.
Nearby signs give directions. Inside, speak to the curator, read both
records, and visit the attendant at the north end. Return to the curator
for your reward. The red floor marker is the exit; normal saving works inside.

Notre-Dame's case unlocks an optional, catchable level-12 Gastly encounter.
Westminster concerns Hoothoot shelter; the Reichstag case concerns a faulty
signal drawing Magnemite. These three visitor areas are fictional Pokemon
interpretations. Other landmarks currently remain exterior sights.

Oak and Ada now connect the regional studies, landmark cases and Celebi
journey around safe routes and keeping companions together. After the
historical London welcome, visit Ada for the conclusion. The Le Havre ferry
now needs dock clearance, and London transport needs the returned luggage;
Oxford reports for those two legs are optional and remain available later.

Trees now use complete silhouettes, including after loading older route
saves. Landmark art has cleaner edges and reduced visual noise. Berlin's
Tiergarten has connected garden paths. The game remains native GBA pixel art.

Use normal CONTINUE with your existing battery save after reopening the
updated ROM. Do not resume an emulator save state made with an older ROM.
Your existing save file is not replaced by the update. See
[case guide and story direction](data/geography/LANDMARK-CASES.md).

## Play the prototype

Open `artifacts/Pokemon-European-Tour-Prototype.gba` in mGBA and choose **NEW
GAME**. The new European Tour title leads into Professor Oak's travel-themed
welcome with Eevee. Complete name entry, then choose your country and first
partner in the bedroom before exploration begins. Use a fresh save to play the
new opening; continuing a game does not replace an existing partner.

For updates, the `Pokemon-European-Tour-Prototype.gba` filename stays the same,
so mGBA can keep using its matching battery `.sav`. If you switch between
versioned ROM filenames, copy your battery save to the matching new name and
keep the original as a backup. Resume through **CONTINUE**; emulator save states
are tied to their ROM build and should not be carried between releases.

| Starting country | Starting city | Choose one first partner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| England | London | Bulbasaur, Cyndaquil, or Totodile |
| France | Paris | Chikorita, Torchic, or Squirtle |
| Germany | Berlin | Treecko, Charmander, or Mudkip |

All starters begin at level 8. Selecting a name shows its sprite and asks for
confirmation. Choose No (or press B) to return to the starter list; B on the
list returns to country selection. Each country offers a Grass, Fire, and
Water choice. These are existing Pokemon assigned to country-specific starter
pools, not new regional forms. The National Pokedex is enabled so the Johto
and Hoenn partners can evolve normally.

You receive 10 Poke Balls, 5 Potions, a Bicycle, and a Town Map. Each starting city has:

- A clinic in the small house northwest of the starting square.
- A train ticket office in the larger building northeast of the square.
- A countryside route through the opening in the northern tree line.

Talk to the gentleman in a station to choose London, Paris, Berlin, Oxford,
Chantilly, or Oranienburg. Travel is free in this milestone. Your home-country choice stays unchanged when traveling.
Clinics heal your party; losing a battle returns you to the clinic of the most
recently visited city. Save through the normal START menu. Use BAG -> KEY ITEMS -> BICYCLE ->
USE to cycle outdoors, or hold the game's B button to run.

## New in v0.9: connected rail journeys

Choose your final destination at any rail desk. The clerk shows the next stop
and how many trains remain, then asks whether to board. No or B leaves you at
the station without starting a journey.

The main line is **London – Paris – Berlin**. Local branches connect Oxford
to London, Chantilly to Paris, and Oranienburg to Berlin. For example, an
Oxford-to-Oranienburg trip calls at London, Paris, and Berlin along the way.
These are game connections, not a real railway timetable.

At each intermediate station you regain control. Speak to its rail clerk to
continue, or leave to explore, heal, shop, or walk. Your planned destination is
included when you use the normal **SAVE** menu, so you can continue after
restarting the game. The clerk recalculates the next connection from whichever
station you visit.

To cancel a booked trip, answer No/B to continuing, then Yes to cancellation.
Answer No/B to cancellation to keep the journey for later. After canceling,
talk to the clerk again to choose another destination. The booking clears on
the final train arrival, or when you reach the destination on foot and speak
to its clerk. Traveling and changing trains remain free.

## The Berlin–Oranienburg journey

Follow Berlin's northern path through German Woodland and continue along
Havel Trail to reach Oranienburg. Both routes have a clear middle path for
walking and cycling. The new trail's optional grass encounters are level 4–6
Sentret, Hoothoot, Teddiursa, and Pineco.

Oranienburg has a park pond, tree-lined walk, clinic, supply shop, and map board.
It is the sixth destination at every rail desk and on the Town Map. Walk south
to return to Berlin, or take a train to any other stop. The expanded rail menu
closes the welcome message before displaying the destination list.

The setting draws on Oranienburg's [woodland and waterside surroundings](https://www.oranienburg.de/Stadtleben/Kultur-Freizeit/Tourismus/).
The game uses existing tiles and compressed distances. Its park and route are
small interpretations, not reproductions of real landmarks or rail services.

The stamp quest still covers London, Paris, and Berlin. A preserved v0.7 Berlin
test save loaded and reached Oranienburg with its Treecko, home country, and
registered Town Map intact.

## The Paris–Chantilly journey

Follow Paris's northern path through the French Gardens and continue through
Chantilly Forest to reach Chantilly. A clear middle path supports walking and
cycling in both directions. Optional grass patches contain level 4–6 Caterpie,
Oddish, Ralts, and Roselia.

Chantilly has a garden walk, clinic, station supply shop, and map board. It is
the fifth destination in every station's menu and on the Town Map. You can
return south to Paris on foot or by Bicycle, or take a train to any other stop.
The town and forest use existing tiles; this is not a recreation of the château.
The garden theme draws on [Chantilly's grounds](https://chateaudechantilly.fr/en/home/).

Chantilly does not add a fourth stamp requirement. The original tour still
covers London, Paris, and Berlin. One preserved v0.6 Paris test save loaded and
reached the new town with its starter, home country, and map registration intact.

## The London–Oxford journey

Follow London's northern path through the English Meadow, continue north along
Oxford Trail, and enter Oxford from the south. Both countryside maps have a
clear middle path for walking or cycling. Optional grass patches on Oxford
Trail contain level 4–6 Pidgey, Mareep, Oddish, and Sentret.

Oxford has a garden square, clinic, station supply shop, and travel-map board.
Its guide explains the return route. Walk south to London or take a train;
Oxford is the fourth destination in every station's menu, including Paris and
Berlin. It is also selectable on the Town Map. These connections compress real
distances into a game route; they do not reproduce real railway services.

The stamp quest still covers London, Paris, and Berlin. Oxford adds a side
journey without changing its three-stamp requirement. Existing players can
reach the new area without restarting. One v0.5 test save was verified loading
and walking to Oxford; broader historical save compatibility is not guaranteed.

## City walks and navigation

London has a southern riverside promenade and a central crossing. Paris has
formal southern gardens, and Berlin has a broad boulevard and a wooded plaza.
Signs name the Thames Promenade, Tuileries Gardens, and Tiergarten Walk.
These are small interpretations made with existing tiles; custom buildings
and detailed landmark artwork remain future work. Each city has its own music.

Open BAG -> KEY ITEMS -> TOWN MAP -> USE for a schematic map of the three
countries and their rail links. Left/right changes the highlighted destination,
A shows rail information, and B or START closes the map. Register the Town Map
from its item menu to open it with SELECT while exploring.

The map board southeast of each station also opens the map. It gives a Town
Map to players who lack one, including continuing players from earlier builds.
The map works in cities, countryside, stations, and clinics, and indicates
your current country. It is a travel overview, not a detailed geographic atlas.

## First objective: the three-city tour

Speak to the woman in London's, Paris's, and Berlin's squares, just southeast of your starting spot.
Each guide awards that city's tour stamp and tells you which stamps remain.
Visit London, Paris, and Berlin in any order. The third guide gives you one
EXP. SHARE, which a Pokemon can hold to gain experience without entering battle.

Stamps and the claimed reward are saved with your game. Talking to a guide
again never duplicates a stamp or reward. If the Items pocket is full, your
stamps remain recorded: free a slot and speak to any guide to claim the reward.
The stamps are tracked through guide dialogue, not a separate Bag item.

## Playable features

- Choose England, France, or Germany on a new game.
- Begin in London, Paris, or Berlin.
- Explore a small city and countryside route in each country.
- Travel between six towns/cities by train, or walk/cycle London–Oxford,
  Paris–Chantilly, and Berlin–Oranienburg.
- Verify battles, healing, saving, loading, and map transitions in mGBA.
- Complete a three-city stamp quest with a one-time EXP. SHARE reward.
- Start with a custom European Tour title and rewritten professor welcome.
- Preview and choose one of three country-specific starters.
- Challenge three local trainers and buy supplies in every station.
- Explore distinct city landscapes and consult a European travel map.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for completed releases and the next milestones.

The twenty-four maps are a functional prototype with shared building/interior art,
not finished recreations of the six towns/cities. The title is a native pixel-art
rail motif with stylized landmarks. The professor portrait, character and
Pokemon sprites and controls guide still come from FireRed. Train
travel uses confirmed station-to-station journeys with saved destinations,
without a moving train sequence. Custom landmark architecture, detailed geographic map art,
more countries, Gyms/story progression, Pokemon riding, additional
generations, and Celebi's WWII storyline remain future work.

## Development on this Windows PC

The source is in `S:\pokemon-europe-version`. Ubuntu under WSL supplies the
build tools. From PowerShell in this folder:

```powershell
.\scripts\build-windows.ps1
```

The output is `pokefirered.gba`. The original compiler is installed in
`tools/agbcc`; its source and setup logs are in the ignored `.local-tools`
directory. `-Compare` is only for the untouched upstream game; a modified
Europe ROM will intentionally have a different checksum.

The wrapper scans source files through Windows Git and compiles in a persistent
Ubuntu cache under `~/.cache/pokemon-europe-build.*`. It copies the ROM and
debugging symbols back to this folder. A Windows-created source archive seeds
new caches; later builds sync changed files and normally take a few seconds.
If the cache is removed, the wrapper recreates it on the next build.

## Testing

The headless integration runner uses the mGBA 0.10.5 core. Tests generate
screenshots and isolated emulator states under `test-output` and never open
the player's normal save file. The original game's known checksum is the
baseline verification before making gameplay changes.

Run the test suite from PowerShell with:

```powershell
.\scripts\test-windows.ps1
```

This PC already has the local mGBA bridge in `.local-tools`. The compiler and
test tools are local setup dependencies, not tracked game assets. The suite
requires the bridge; `scripts/build-test-runner.sh` rebuilds it from the local
mGBA source checkout. See `TESTING.md` for test coverage and limitations.

## USA companion

Compatibility is a design objective, not an implemented feature. The two
projects need agreement on engine, Pokemon/item/move IDs, save data, and link
protocol. Visiting maps in a separate ROM requires a deliberate transfer
mechanism or a combined build; standard Pokemon trading does not transfer
the player into another game's world.

## England: Oak's field study (v0.11)

Speak to Oak's aide on the west side of London's square. Accept his field
study, defeat Oliver in English Meadow and Alice on Oxford Trail, then speak
to your rival on the west side of Oxford's square. Earlier trail wins count,
and travelers from France or Germany can join too.

The rival uses Pidgey (level 7) and Eevee (level 8). Heal at the clinic and
bring Potions before accepting the match. Declining leaves the rival ready
for later; losing sends you to your last visited clinic and allows a retry.
After winning, return to the London aide for one Soothe Bell. Let a Pokemon
hold it to help friendship grow. A full Items pocket leaves the reward
waiting with the aide, and normal saving preserves the chapter's progress.

Your rival uses the name chosen during the opening. This chapter introduces
England's story and unlocks its first Gym.

## Oxford Gym (v0.12)

After finishing Oak's field study and delivering your report to the London
aide, visit the Gym north of Oxford's square. Ellis specializes in Rock:
Geodude is level 10 and Onix is level 12. The advice NPC explains preparation;
Grass and Water moves are useful, and Oddish can be caught on the English
trails. Heal at the clinic and bring Potions.

Win to receive the Boulderbadge immediately, shown in the first slot of your
Trainer Card. Ellis also gives one TM39 (Rock Tomb), with a TM Case added if
needed. If there is no room, you keep your badge and can return for the TM.
Declining leaves the challenge available; losing returns you to your most
recent clinic and lets you retry. Repeat conversations never repeat the
battle prize or TM. Save normally to retain both rewards.

This prototype uses an existing Gym interior, building tiles, leader artwork,
and the original first-badge graphics and engine effects. Ellis and the Oxford
progression are new. Chantilly now adds the second Gym; later country Gyms remain planned.

## France: the garden survey (v0.13)

After earning your first badge, speak to Celine west of the Paris city guide.
Accept her survey, then read the two study markers beside the main paths:
one in French Gardens north of Paris, and one in the central clearing of
Chantilly Forest. Visit them in either order. Celine and Remy give directions
for whichever notes are still missing.

Take both observations to Remy, west of the Chantilly town guide. After he
reviews them, return to Celine in Paris for one Miracle Seed. A Pokemon
holding it gains power for its Grass-type moves. If the Items pocket is
full, the reviewed report and reward remain waiting. Normal saving preserves
each observation, the review, and the claimed reward. Repeated marker visits
or conversations do not reset progress or give another reward.

This exploration quest unlocks the French Gym. Existing travel, trainers,
and regional Rare Candy rewards remain available throughout the survey.

## France: Chantilly Gym (v0.14)

After collecting Celine's Miracle Seed, visit the Gym north of Chantilly's
square. Follow the bridges across the pool to Marine. The Gym guide describes
her Water-type team: Psyduck at level 12 and Horsea at level 14. Grass and
Electric moves help; heal your team and bring Potions before challenging her.

Winning immediately awards the Cascadebadge in the second Trainer Card slot,
plus one TM03 (Water Pulse). Your Boulderbadge remains intact. If the TM stack
or the space needed for a missing TM Case is full, the badge stays earned and
Marine holds the TM until you make room. Declining or losing leaves the battle
available, and losing returns you to the local clinic. Normal Save/Continue
preserves the battle result and any pending reward.

This prototype reuses the pool Gym interior, swimmer artwork, and original
Cascadebadge graphics and engine effects. Marine's battle and quest connection
are new. Germany's signal-repair delivery follows this chapter.

## Germany: signal-repair delivery (v0.15)

With both Gym badges, speak to Lena west of Berlin's city guide. Accept her
repair parcel and take it to Karl, west of Oranienburg's town guide. You can
use the railway or the existing woodland and Havel walking routes. Karl
repairs a trail beacon and gives you a report to return to Lena.

Lena rewards the completed delivery with one Magnet, a held item that boosts
Electric-type moves. A full Items pocket leaves the report ready for a later
claim. Repeated conversations do not reset the delivery or duplicate rewards.
The parcel and report are tracked as quest progress outside the Bag; normal
Save/Continue preserves them. This is a short dialogue-and-travel chapter,
using existing character artwork. The completed delivery unlocks Oranienburg Gym.

## Germany: Oranienburg Gym (v0.16)

After collecting Lena's Magnet, visit the Gym north of Oranienburg's square.
Conrad uses Electric-type partners: Voltorb at level 14 and Pikachu at level 16.
Ground moves work well, and Grass partners resist Electric attacks. Heal at
the clinic and bring Potions; Water and Flying partners face a harder match.

Win for the Thunderbadge in your Trainer Card's third slot and one TM34
(Shock Wave). Shock Wave bypasses accuracy checks, but Ground types are immune.
The TM can be used once. If there is no space for the TM or a missing TM Case,
Conrad keeps it until you make room. The badge remains earned. Losing returns
you to the local clinic; declining leaves the battle available. Save normally
to preserve the result and any pending reward.

Conrad uses existing scientist artwork and a reused Gym interior. The original
badge graphics and engine effects remain. This completes a prototype story
and Gym arc in each of the three starting countries. The first additional transportation option is the riverboat link below.

## Riverboats: London and Oxford (v0.17)

After earning the Thunderbadge from Conrad, speak to the sailor at the
southeastern riverside landing in London or Oxford. From the town guide,
head east and then toward the river. The sign beside the captain identifies
the destination. Each free trip goes directly to the other town; choose No
or press B to stay where you are. Your existing rail itinerary remains booked,
and the next rail clerk recalculates its remaining route from that station.

You can board with your Bicycle and save normally at either landing. Home
country, party, money, quest progress, and rewards carry across the trip.
Earlier trains and walking routes remain available before the boat unlocks.
This is a prototype riverboat service represented by a captain, a confirmation,
and an arrival transition, using existing riverbank tiles. There is no moving
boat scene yet. Rental Lapras rides are available as described below; coastal ferries now connect Dover and Calais.

## Rental Lapras rides (v0.18)

After earning the Thunderbadge, speak to the swimming instructor just east
of the riverboat captain in London or Oxford. She offers a free Lapras rental
and shows its picture before you confirm. Steer with the direction buttons
and move onto a clear riverbank to dismount. You can rent again at either
instructor. A Bicycle is put away when you mount; you return to walking ashore.

Rentals need neither Surf nor a free party slot. Your Pokemon, money, home
country, quests, and saved rail itinerary are preserved. You can save while
riding, continue on the water, then dismount and resume your travels.

This first riding prototype uses the original generic Surf rider graphics
and movement, introduced by a Lapras rental dialogue and picture. It does not
yet have a custom Lapras rider sprite, land mounts, or a long water route.
The playable water areas are the existing town riverside basins.

## Coastal ferries: Dover and Calais (v0.19)

With the Thunderbadge, speak to the coach attendant at the back of London
or Paris station. London connects to Dover Port; Paris connects to Calais
Port. Speak to the captain to cross the Channel for free. No or B cancels
without changing your location or saved rail trip. The north port exit takes
the return coach to the station entrance in its home city.

Dover and Calais appear as two additional travel-map stops. Select either and
press A for ferry/coach information. They are reached by coach and ferry;
the rail destination menu still serves the original six cities. Existing rail
bookings survive coach and ferry detours, and the next rail clerk recalculates
the remaining journey. Home country, party, money, badges, and quests persist.
Save normally in either port and continue your crossing later.

Both ports reuse the existing harbor and ferry artwork. These are compact
transport stops with transition-based crossings, not full Dover/Calais towns
or animated sea voyages. Each port uses its connected city's clinic respawn.
This completes the first transportation prototype pass. The Celebi opening
chapter follows below. Custom mounts and larger routes remain polish.


## Celebi: a memory in the forest (v0.20)

After earning the Thunderbadge, speak to Ada, the researcher two spaces east
of Oxford's town guide. Accept her investigation, take the train to Chantilly,
and walk south into Chantilly Forest. Celebi waits just west of the middle
path, a little south of the forest survey sign. Speak to it and choose to
listen. You see a brief vision of a station platform in 1940, then record it.

Return to Ada in Oxford to review the sighting and receive one Rare Candy.
If the Items pocket is full, she keeps the reward and your report until you
make room. Declining either invitation leaves it available for later. Normal
Save/Continue preserves each stage, and repeated conversations cannot duplicate
the reward or reset your previous regional quests.

This is the present-day prologue: Celebi uses its existing overworld and
portrait artwork, and the vision is told through dialogue. There is no capture
or battle in this prologue. The first playable visit
to the past follows below; the larger WWII story remains in development.


## Celebi: the first visit to 1940 (v0.21)

After collecting Ada's prologue reward, return to Celebi in Chantilly Forest.
It now offers to take you to a small station refuge in Chantilly in 1940.
Choose Yes to travel. Celebi waits beside your arrival spot throughout the
visit: speak to it whenever you want to return to the present-day forest.
Choosing No or pressing B keeps you where you are.

Speak to Elise, the child in the courtyard, then ask the keeper beside her for
a blanket. Bring it back to Elise to warm her frightened Eevee. This is a short
fictional scene set during the war, using existing game artwork. It is not a
reconstruction of a documented station or historical event. Larger historical
locations and the rest of the wartime story are still to come.

You can leave midway, save in either era, and resume later. The blanket and
past task are tracked separately from your Bag and present-day quests. Your
party, items, money, badges, home country and booked rail destination carry
through the trip. Cycling ends on arrival in the refuge. There are no battles
or random encounters in this first historical area.

The Town Map identifies your location as France, 1940, labels its schematic
as a present-day map, and reminds you to return through Celebi. The historical
refuge is not a rail destination. Use normal Save/Continue when updating from
v0.20; its completed prologue unlocks the new trip immediately.


## Historical station news (v0.22)

After warming Elise's Eevee with the blanket, speak to the guide in the
southeast corner of the 1940 refuge. He takes you to a nearby station post.
Read the notice northeast of the dispatcher, then speak to the dispatcher to
confirm the news. Return with the local guide and report to the refuge keeper.
Elise and Ada have new dialogue after you deliver the confirmed message.

Both guides accept No or B. Celebi waits by the arrival point at the station
post as well as at the refuge, so you can return to the present from either
area. Re-entering the past brings you to the refuge; visit the post again to
resume any unfinished work. Normal Save/Continue preserves the notice,
confirmation and delivered report independently of the earlier blanket task.
The objective does not use Bag space or consume supplies.

This continues the fictional wartime story with a short news-gathering task.
The post reuses the refuge courtyard artwork. The first train departure follows below; further historical destinations
remain in development. Saves made in the v0.21
refuge are supported; the newly added guide is restored when loading them.


## Historical train to Beauvais (v0.23)

After delivering the station-news report to the refuge keeper, return to the
1940 station post. The dispatcher now announces a train to Beauvais. Read the
same notice board to receive the boarding offer. Choose Yes to travel; No or B
leaves you at the post. The journey uses dialogue and a map transition, without
an animated train scene.

At Beauvais reception, speak to the host near the back to check Elise and Eevee
in together, then speak to Elise on the right. She now stays at reception and
no longer appears in the old refuge. Ada acknowledges their arrival after you
finish check-in and return to Oxford.

Celebi beside the reception entrance returns you directly to the present-day
Chantilly Forest. The south exit instead takes the return service to the
historical station post. You can revisit Beauvais, leave before check-in, save
in either era, and finish later without resetting progress. Your present-day
party, supplies and booked rail itinerary are preserved by these trips.

The Town Map names Beauvais as your historical location. Its existing diagram
remains a present-day travel reference, with Chantilly as the entry point for
these past visits; Beauvais is not added to the modern rail menu. The reception
reuses an existing interior as placeholder artwork. This is a fictional journey
within the game's wartime story, not a documented historical train service.


## A message home and the first account (v0.24)

After checking Elise and Eevee in at Beauvais, speak to Elise again. After
showing you Eevee, she asks whether you can tell the old refuge keeper that
they arrived together and have shelter. Yes accepts; No or B leaves the request
available for later. Take the south return service to the station post, then
ask its guide to return to the refuge and speak to the keeper.

Bring the keeper's reply back to Elise at Beauvais. Then use Celebi to return
to the present and tell Ada in Oxford. She records your first historical
account and awards one Luxury Ball. If the Poke Balls pocket cannot hold it,
she keeps the account and reward ready until you make room. Repeated reports
cannot award another ball.

Messages use saved quest progress rather than Bag items. You can leave either
era or save midway and resume. The keeper and Elise remember the completed
exchange, and you can keep visiting the historical stops after Ada records it.
This closes the first short historical arc; the wider wartime story remains
in development.

## Beauvais supplies and a place to rest (v0.25)

After Ada records your first historical account and gives the Luxury Ball,
return through Celebi and take the train to Beauvais. Speak to the reception
host at the back of the room. Accept the request to collect a care parcel,
take the south return service, and speak to the dispatcher at the station post.
Read the board to return to Beauvais and give the parcel to the host.

The delivery opens the reception's care corner. Speak to the host again and
choose Yes to restore your entire party's HP, status and move PP for free.
You can rest as often as you need. No or B declines without healing, and the
service stays available. Delivering the parcel alone does not heal the party.

The parcel uses saved quest progress and takes no Bag space. You can save,
visit the present or take another train without losing it. No or B at the
initial request leaves it available for later. Earlier quests and Celebi's
return points remain available. The care corner uses the existing reception
interior; further historical locations and chapters are still planned.

## Beauvais garden and Luc's Pidgey (v0.26)

After delivering the care parcel, speak to Elise at Beauvais reception. She
offers a visit to the garden. No or B leaves you in reception; you can ask
again later. The garden is a new small outdoor layout with quiet paths,
flower beds and complete forest borders, using the existing tiles and sprites.

Speak to Luc on the west side and accept his request. Learn his whistle, then
approach Pidgey beside the northeastern trees. Return to Luc to reunite them.
Pidgey then stays beside him, and both remain available to talk to. The bird
will not leave with you before you learn the tune. This is a story reunion;
Pidgey does not join your battle party or use a Bag slot.

The guide north of your arrival point takes you back to reception, where the
host can still heal your party. Celebi beside the south path returns you to
the present. After a present-day detour, use the refuge, station post and
Beauvais train again, then ask Elise to revisit the garden. Search progress
and the reunion survive saving, leaving and returning. The Town Map identifies
your location as Beauvais, 1940 while showing the present-day travel diagram.

## Story journal (v0.27)

Open the Town Map from Bag -> Key Items, then press SELECT to read the Celebi
journey journal. The current lead names your next objective, the person to
speak to and where to go. It follows your saved progress from preparing for
the first sighting through the refuge, Beauvais, supply run and Pidgey reunion.
Pending reward leads remind you which Bag pocket needs room.

Press A to switch between the current lead and seven completed milestones.
Press SELECT to return to the travel map without changing your selected stop.
B or START closes the map/journal. These controls also work if you register
the Town Map and open it with SELECT from the field: press SELECT again once
the map is open to enter the journal.

The journal reads your existing save, so you do not need to restart or revisit
completed quests. Reading it does not accept requests, claim rewards, change
your party or book travel. It reopens on the travel map each time. The journal
covers the Celebi storyline. The regional topic below covers Gyms and stamps.

## Regional journal topic (v0.28)

While the journal is open, press Up or Down to switch between the Celebi
journey and the Europe tour. A still switches between the current lead and
milestones, and SELECT returns to the map. Up/Down keeps the current page
type when changing topics. Reopening the journal starts with the Celebi lead.
The shoulder buttons retain FireRed's existing Help behavior.

The Europe topic follows the England field study, both required trail trainers,
the Oxford rival, all three regional story arcs and their Gyms. It points to
unclaimed Gym TMs and other story rewards when Bag space is needed. After the
three Gyms and their TMs, it points to missing city stamps and the EXP. SHARE
reward. Stamps remain optional and can be earned earlier, in any order.

The regional milestones show the three city stamps, claimed EXP. SHARE and
three badges independently. A badge remains marked done even if its TM is
still pending. Both journal topics read the same existing save without changing
requests, rewards, Pokémon, items or travel plans.

## Onward to Amiens (v0.29)

After reuniting Luc and Pidgey, return from the garden to Beauvais reception,
take its south service to the Chantilly station post, and speak to the
dispatcher. He now offers a train to Amiens. No or B leaves you at the post.
The noticeboard still offers the existing Beauvais train, so both destinations
remain available. Travel is free and preserves your present-day rail booking.

In Amiens, speak to Nora beside the west courtyard path. Read the notice by
the northeast trees, then return to her to confirm the meeting instructions.
Reading the board before Nora's welcome does not skip the briefing. Once she
confirms your notes, the journal records the eighth historical milestone.

The guide north of your arrival point returns you directly to the Chantilly
post. Celebi beside the south path returns you to the present. You can leave
before finishing, save midway and resume later; ask the dispatcher to revisit
Amiens. Your earlier friends remain in Beauvais. The Town Map identifies
Amiens, 1940 and the journal gives each new objective.

This is a fictional onward journey and reception stop within the prototype,
not a recreation of a documented train service. The courtyard reuses the
existing garden tile set and tree borders with a lighter central layout.
Further Amiens story content remains planned.


## Mira and Meowth (v0.30)

After confirming the Amiens meeting instructions, speak to Nora again to
help with a reunion. No or B leaves the task available for later. Compare
Mira's description on the north path with the porter's report on the east
side; either order works. Bring both reports to Nora, then give Mira the
news. Meowth will stay beside her after the reunion.

The Town Map journal guides each stage and records a ninth historical
milestone. You can save midway, take the return train or visit the present
with Celebi, then resume. Saves made in v0.29 Amiens load the new characters
without requiring you to leave the courtyard first. Use normal Continue
with your battery save when updating ROMs, rather than an old save state.
This task does not add a Pokemon to your party or require Bag space.


## Amiens party care (v0.31)

After reuniting Mira and Meowth, speak to Nora on the west courtyard path.
She now offers free, repeatable care for your entire party: HP, status and
move PP are restored, including fainted Pokemon. Choose No or press B to
leave your team's health unchanged. The completed-reunion journal lead
points you to this service.

An existing save with the reunion completed already qualifies. Care stays
available after saving, taking the return service or traveling with Celebi.
No Bag space or further delivery is required.


## Recording the Amiens account (v0.32)

Once Mira and Meowth are reunited, return to the present with Celebi and
visit Ada in Oxford's square. She offers to record your account of the
reunion. Choose Yes to complete the report, or No/B to leave it for later.
The story journal points to Ada and records a tenth historical milestone
when you share the account. Repeated conversations acknowledge the report.

This follows directly from existing reunion saves. There is no item reward
or delivery requirement. Nora's free party care and both Amiens return
routes remain available, so you can revisit everyone after recording it.


## Amiens travel bulletin (v0.33)

After recording the reunion with Ada, return to Amiens and speak to the
porter on the east side. Accept his request, read the northeast notice,
then bring your notes back to him for verification. Finally, speak to Nora
on the west path to pass on the checked instructions. No/B at the porter's
offer leaves the task available for later. Reading early or speaking to
Nora before verification does not skip the required steps.

The bulletin confirms a waiting plan: groups stay together in the courtyard
until called. No onward service is available in this version. The existing
return train, Celebi travel and Nora's free party care remain available.
The journal guides all stages; you can save or leave partway and resume.
This task adds no item requirement or reward and no additional checklist row.


## Onward to Rouen (v0.34)

After delivering the checked Amiens bulletin to Nora, speak to the porter
on the east side. The onward service is now confirmed: he offers a free
train to Rouen. No or B leaves you in Amiens. The Amiens notice updates to

In Rouen, speak to Leon beside the west courtyard path to record your
arrival. The northeast notice explains the reception stop. The guide north
of your arrival point returns directly to Amiens, where Nora's party care
remains available. Celebi beside your arrival returns you to the present.
You can leave before the welcome, save and resume later. Revisit through
the Amiens porter. Your present-day rail booking is preserved.

The journal guides boarding, meeting Leon and return travel. The map labels
your location as Rouen, 1940 while retaining the present-day travel diagram.
This is a fictional prototype journey, not a documented historical service.
The initial v0.34 stop reused the Amiens courtyard. Version 0.35 replaces
that shared layout with the riverside described below. Additional Rouen
architecture and story content remain future work.


## Rouen riverside (v0.35)

Rouen now has its own wider courtyard, with paved paths leading east to
waterfront banks and a crossing. From the arrival point, follow the paved
path east, then north to the crossing. You can explore the far bank and
walk back to Leon. Water and the outer forest block walking.

The noticeboard mentions the new path. All existing NPC, noticeboard and
arrival coordinates are preserved. Every previously reachable ground tile
remains reachable. Older Rouen battery saves automatically select the new
layout on Continue, with their position and story progress preserved.
Use normal Continue rather than an old emulator save state when updating.


## Leon's route book (v0.36)

After Leon records your Rouen arrival, speak to him again to help find the
reception route book. No or B leaves the request available. Once accepted,
follow the east path to the paved crossing, cross to the far bank and head
north. Face the pickup beside the water and press A, then bring it back to
Leon on the west courtyard path.

The wrapped book travels with your quest notes; it does not need Bag space
and is not added as a usable inventory item. It disappears after collection
and stays collected through return travel, Celebi trips and saving. The
journal guides the request, search, return and completed task. Existing
Rouen saves load the new pickup correctly without leaving the map first.


## Rouen party care (v0.37)

Returning the route book now unlocks free, repeatable party care with Leon.
Speak to him again and choose Yes to restore the whole party's HP, status
and move PP, including fainted Pokemon. No or B leaves health unchanged.
The book hand-in itself does not heal automatically.

Existing saves with the book already returned qualify immediately. Care
remains available after saving, revisiting through Amiens or traveling with
Celebi. The journal's completed route-book entry points to the service.
No Bag space or additional delivery is needed.

## Historical Le Havre reception (v0.39)

After returning Leon's route book, speak to the transport clerk southeast
of Leon, near Rouen's riverside path. He offers a free connection to Le Havre.
No or B leaves you in Rouen. The new clerk appears immediately in older
Rouen saves; no restart or map exit is needed.

At Le Havre, speak to the captain directly below your arrival point. He
records your arrival and offers a return service to Rouen. Decline to stay
at the port, or accept to return. Celebi on your left returns you to the

choose SIMPLE/SHADED regional map detail, NATURAL/HIGH CONTRAST map colors,
