## Detour to another station (v1.09)

Your booking stays active when you walk to a different town. Speak to its
rail clerk: the next stop and trains remaining are calculated from that
station. YES boards that connection. Decline cancellation to keep the trip
for later; your final destination stays the same.

## Finish a journey on foot (v1.08)

You can walk the final leg of a booked journey. Speak to the rail clerk
at your destination to complete it and clear the booking. The clerk names
the destination and explains how to choose another trip. Speak again to
open the stop list; your completed booking stays cleared after Continue.

## Resume after exploring (v1.07)

The rail clerk shows your saved destination, next stop and trains remaining.
YES boards the next train. NO (or B) opens the cancellation choice;
choose NO there to keep your booking and continue exploring.

## Explore during a rail journey (v1.06)

Intermediate boarding names your next stop and the trip's final destination.
At intermediate stops, read the station wall notice for local walks.
Your booking stays while you explore, including through Save/Continue.
Return to the rail clerk and confirm continuing your journey to travel on.

## Rail booking cues (v1.05)

The rail clerk now explains the booking controls before opening the stop
list. Choose a stop to preview the route; B or Exit leaves the list.

## Development on this Windows PC

The source is in `S:\pokemon-europe-version`. Ubuntu under WSL supplies the
build tools. From PowerShell in this folder:

```powershell
.\scripts\build-windows.ps1
```

The output is `pokefirered.gba`. The original compiler is installed in
`tools/agbcc`; its source and setup logs are in the ignored `.local-tools`
directory. `-Compare` is only for the untouched upstream game; a modified
Europe ROM will intentionally have a different checksum.

The wrapper scans source files through Windows Git and compiles in a persistent
Ubuntu cache under `~/.cache/pokemon-europe-build.*`. It copies the ROM and
debugging symbols back to this folder. A Windows-created source archive seeds
new caches; later builds sync changed files and normally take a few seconds.
If the cache is removed, the wrapper recreates it on the next build.

## Testing

The headless integration runner uses the mGBA 0.10.5 core. Tests generate
screenshots and isolated emulator states under `test-output` and never open
the player's normal save file. The original game's known checksum is the
baseline verification before making gameplay changes.

Run the test suite from PowerShell with:

```powershell
.\scripts\test-windows.ps1
```

This PC already has the local mGBA bridge in `.local-tools`. The compiler and
test tools are local setup dependencies, not tracked game assets. The suite
requires the bridge; `scripts/build-test-runner.sh` rebuilds it from the local
mGBA source checkout. See `TESTING.md` for test coverage and limitations.

## USA companion

Compatibility is a design objective, not an implemented feature. The two
projects need agreement on engine, Pokemon/item/move IDs, save data, and link
protocol. Visiting maps in a separate ROM requires a deliberate transfer
mechanism or a combined build; standard Pokemon trading does not transfer
the player into another game's world.

## Le Havre dock instructions (v0.40)

After the captain welcomes you, speak to the dockworker on the upper quay,
to the right of the closed building entrance. Accept his request, read the
notice on that entrance, then confirm the instructions with the captain.
The journal tracks each step and records completion as its fifteenth
historical milestone. The captain still offers the Rouen return after the
confirmation; declining travel keeps your completed task.

You can decline the request, revisit each conversation, save at any stage,
or return through Celebi and continue later. Existing Le Havre saves gain
the dockworker immediately. There is still no onward sailing destination.


## Le Havre party care (v0.41)

After confirming the dock instructions with the captain, speak to the
dockworker again for free, repeatable party care. Accept to restore the
whole team's HP, status and move PP, including fainted Pokemon. No or B
leaves the party unchanged. The captain announces the service when you
complete the task; the journal's completed lead also points to it.

Existing completed saves unlock care immediately. Both return routes remain
available, and the service stays unlocked when you revisit Le Havre.


## Recording the port visit (v0.42)

Once the captain has confirmed the dock instructions, return through Celebi
and visit Ada in Oxford. She offers to record your Le Havre visit. Accept
to add the sixteenth historical journal milestone, or choose No/B and
report later. The journal guides you to Ada and marks the finished account.

The report preserves your party, items and previous quest progress. You can
save before or after it, revisit Le Havre and continue using dockworker
care. Existing completed dock-task saves can report immediately. This closes
the port reception chapter. The later Southampton crossing and London 1940 chapters are now playable.


## Direct port revisits (v0.43)

After Ada records your port account, speak to Celebi in present-day
Chantilly Forest. Accept the offer to travel, then choose REFUGE for the
original Chantilly arrival or LE HAVRE to arrive directly beside the port
captain. EXIT or B at the destination menu keeps you in the present.
Declining the initial travel offer also keeps you in the forest.

Existing recorded-account saves unlock this choice immediately. Before the
account is recorded, Celebi follows the original refuge route. Neither
destination changes your quest progress or modern rail booking. Le Havre's
care, Rouen return service and Celebi return remain available. Ada and the
completed journal lead explain the new shortcut.


## Southampton reception (v0.44)

After recording the port account with Ada, revisit Le Havre and speak to
the ferry clerk on the upper quay, left of the closed entrance. Accept his
free crossing to Southampton. No or B leaves you in Le Havre. The clerk
appears immediately in older Le Havre saves.

At Southampton, speak to the host directly below your arrival point to
record your welcome and earn the seventeenth journal milestone. He offers
a return to Le Havre; declining keeps you at reception. Celebi to your left
returns you to present-day Chantilly Forest at any stage. The north entrance
is closed. Dockworker care remains available when you return to Le Havre.

The journal tracks departure, arrival and welcome, including saved unfinished
visits. The map labels Southampton in 1940 and uses London as its modern
England reference. Your existing progress and rail itinerary stay saved.
This is a fictional prototype crossing and reception scene using existing
harbor art, not a recreation of a documented historical sailing service.


## The traveler's luggage (v0.45)

After the Southampton host welcomes you, speak to the reception worker on
the upper quay, right of the closed entrance. Accept the request to find a
green-labeled bag. The pickup appears on the left side of the upper quay.
Collect it and return to the worker to finish the task and earn the
eighteenth historical journal milestone. No or B declines the request.

The luggage is tracked as quest progress, so a full Bag does not prevent
the task. It disappears after collection and stays gone after completion.
You can save or leave through either return route while the task is active,
then resume later. The journal guides each stage. Existing Southampton saves
gain the worker and pickup logic immediately, without restarting the game.

and move PP, including fainted Pokemon. No or B leaves the party unchanged.

journal lead also points to it.

## London reception (v0.49)

After Ada records the Southampton account, return to Southampton and speak
to the transport clerk directly below the care worker on the upper quay.
Accept the free onward trip to London's reception courtyard. No or B leaves
you at Southampton. The clerk appears immediately in older saves.

At London reception, speak to Rose on the left side of the courtyard to
record your arrival and earn the twentieth historical journal milestone.
The guide above your arrival offers a return to Southampton; Celebi to your
left returns you to present-day Chantilly Forest. Both exits remain open
before and after Rose's welcome. The courtyard sign also points to Rose.

You can save during unfinished visits and resume later. The map labels this
as London in 1940, separately from modern London, and your rail booking and
previous progress remain saved. Southampton care remains available on
return. This is a fictional prototype reception journey using the established
garden artwork, not a reconstruction of a documented wartime service.


## World Options and geographic map (v0.50)

Read a town map board to receive WORLD OPTIONS in Key Items. Open it to
choose SIMPLE/SHADED regional map detail, NATURAL/HIGH CONTRAST map colors,
and ORIGINAL/RED/LEAF overworld avatar. Up/Down selects a row; Left/Right or
A changes it; B/START returns. You can register it to SELECT. Use the normal
Save command to keep your settings. Your trainer identity is unchanged.

The travel map now projects real coastlines and approximate city centers.
Map detail currently affects this regional screen only. Town walking maps
are still prototypes; their geographic and architectural redesign is the
active roadmap priority. Avatar customization currently offers presets.
