# Pokemon European Tour complete walkthrough

This walkthrough covers the **v1.15 story route**, verified from a fresh game,
from country selection
through Rose's welcome in London in 1940 and Ada's concluding account in Oxford.
It contains story spoilers. The main route below includes every hand-in needed
to reach that ending. Optional activities follow it.

Fresh v1.15 checks cover full England/Bulbasaur, France/Chikorita and
Germany/Mudkip routes, including ending Continue and deferred accounts.
The three optional landmark cases and combined synthesis were also checked
from the completed Germany save. The optional stamp tour was also checked after story and case completion.
The optional Gastly was also caught using earned Great Balls, followed by
Save/Continue. Other starter teams are outside those full runs. See TESTING.md for exact coverage.

The optional riverboat, rental Lapras and present-day coastal trips were
also checked after completion. The archived suites record 72 passing checks
across nine runs; these include starting-connection checks and are not 72
separate playthroughs. See TESTING.md for evidence limits.

In v1.16, the capital Rooms map pages also explain entry with A and the
south-door exit. The story route and room controls are unchanged; the full
playthrough evidence above is from v1.15, with focused v1.16 map/save checks.

## Controls and directions

Use the D-pad to move, A to speak, read and confirm, and B to back out. Keep
pressing A to finish every page of a conversation. For a YES/NO question, select
YES to accept the task or journey described in the steps below. Most refusals
leave the task available for later.

North is up; south is down. Coordinates in parentheses identify tiles, measured
from the upper-left corner of the current map, starting at zero. They are a
reference if two people or signs look similar; the game does not display them.
An **approach** coordinate is where you stand. Face the stated direction and
press A. Follow paved paths around buildings, water and trees rather than
walking a straight line through obstacles.

Read the travel board near a town station first, at (19,11), approaching
from (19,12) and facing north. It gives you the TOWN MAP and story journal
if you do not already have them. Open the BAG, choose KEY ITEMS and use
the TOWN MAP for travel guidance. On the map, A shows routes, R shows Places,
and L switches capital Places pages to
Rooms. SELECT opens the story journal. The journal's current lead is useful
when returning to an unfinished quest.

Use START, SAVE and confirm the save prompts before stopping. Reopen the same
ROM with its save file and choose CONTINUE. Your quests and rail bookings are
saved. An emulator save state from another ROM version is not a substitute for
the game's normal Save/Continue.

## Start a new game

1. Choose NEW GAME, finish Oak's introduction, and choose your character and names.
2. Choose a home country, then a starter. Confirm the starter preview with YES.
3. You begin in that country's capital with a level 8 starter.

| Home country | Starting city | Starter choices | First destination for this route |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| England | London | Bulbasaur, Cyndaquil, Totodile | Stay in London |
| France | Paris | Chikorita, Torchic, Squirtle | Take the train to London |
| Germany | Berlin | Treecko, Charmander, Mudkip | Take the train through Paris to London |

The home choice does not change the required badge order. All three starts
can visit England immediately. Bulbasaur, Chikorita or Treecko gives you a
useful Grass-type partner for the first two Gyms. Other starters can finish the same
quests; train and catch additional partners when a Gym has a type advantage.

## Travel and team preparation

Every present-day town has the same basic service positions:

| Service or person | Where to go | Interaction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rail station | Door at (23,9), approach (23,10) | Walk north through the door |
| Rail clerk inside | Approach (7,7) | Face north, press A |
| Supplies clerk inside | Approach (1,7) | Face north; choose BUY |
| Clinic | Door at (6,9), approach (6,10) | Enter and speak to the nurse at the back |
| City or town guide | Approach (16,14) | Face south |
| Regional quest NPC | Approach (10,14) | Face south |
| Gym in Oxford, Chantilly or Oranienburg | Door at (15,9), approach (15,10) | Walk north through the door |

In v1.15, the framed wall picture inside Oxford, Chantilly and Oranienburg
stations is readable from (9,2), facing north. It points toward the Gym,
local quest contact and route south. These notices are optional directions;
read the quest steps below for the requirements to challenge each Gym leader.

At the rail clerk, finish the welcome, select your **final destination**, read
the preview and choose YES to board. At an intermediate station, speak to the
clerk again and choose YES to continue. The booking finishes at the selected
destination. Leave a station through the middle south exit: from the arrival
tile (4,7), walk down twice. You emerge at (23,10).

The network is Oxford--London--Paris--Berlin--Oranienburg, with Chantilly
branching from Paris. For example, London to Chantilly stops in Paris; Oxford
to Oranienburg stops in London, Paris and Berlin. You can explore between
trains. If you decline continuing, the clerk asks separately whether to cancel;
choose NO at that second question to keep the booking. A walking detour changes
the next connection, not your booked destination.

Heal before each required battle. Clinics are free. Station shops sell Potions,
Antidotes, Paralyze Heals, Repels and Poke Balls; a Potion costs 300. Keep some
Potions for battles and enough free Bag space for rewards. If a reward is
pending because a pocket is full, make room and speak to the giver again.

If you chose a Fire starter and need help against Ellis, buy Poke Balls and
walk in the grass in English Meadow or Oxford Trail until you find **Oddish**.
In the wild battle, weaken it without knocking it out, then choose BAG,
POKE BALLS, POKE BALL and USE. Train it in wild battles until it can survive
Ellis's level 10 and 12 team, healing between fights; its Grass attack is useful
against both Rock/Ground opponents and Marine's Water team. **Mareep** also
appears on those English routes and can provide Electric attacks for Marine
once trained. Catching these partners is optional if your existing team wins.
Wild encounters require grass; the central paved walking lanes are safe.

The straight central paths connect each capital to its northern branch town:

| Start | Walk north through | Destination |
| --- | --- | --- |
| London | English Meadow, then Oxford Trail | Oxford |
| Paris | French Gardens, then Chantilly Forest | Chantilly |
| Berlin | German Woodland, then Havel Trail | Oranienburg |

Walking these routes is useful for required observations and training. Otherwise,
the trains are the simplest way to follow the town-to-town instructions.

## Complete England and earn the first badge

1. In London, speak to **Oak's aide**, west of the city guide. Stand at (10,14),
   face south and accept the field study with YES.
2. Leave London to the north along the central path. In **English Meadow**,
   speak to **Oliver** from (17,19), facing north. Accept the battle and win.
3. Heal at the London clinic, then continue north through the meadow into
   **Oxford Trail**. Speak to **Alice** from (17,19), facing north, and win.
   Earlier victories against Oliver or Alice count if you had not accepted
   the study yet.
4. Continue north to **Oxford**. Heal, then speak to your rival west of the
   town guide, from (10,14), facing south. Accept and win the match against
   Pidgey level 7 and Eevee level 8.
5. Return to **London** and speak to Oak's aide again. Collect the **Soothe
   Bell**. This report is required: beating the rival alone does not unlock
   the first Gym.
6. Go to **Oxford**, heal and enter the Gym north of the square. From the
   indoor arrival tile (6,14), walk north eight tiles to (6,6). Face north
   and speak to **Ellis**. Choose YES and win.

Ellis uses **Geodude level 10** and **Onix level 12**. Grass and Water attacks
are useful. If your starter lacks them, train or catch a suitable partner
before attempting the fight; healing items help but do not remove a type
disadvantage.

**Completion check:** the Trainer Card shows the **Boulderbadge**. You also
receive the TM Case and **TM39 Rock Tomb**, subject to Bag capacity. The badge
is awarded when you win even if the TM has to be collected later. Save.

## Complete the French survey and earn the second badge

1. Take the train to **Paris**. Speak to **Celine**, west of the city guide,
   from (10,14), facing south. With the first badge earned, choose YES to
   accept the garden survey.
2. Walk north into **French Gardens**. Read the study marker at (13,16),
   standing at (13,17) and facing north. Finish the conversation that records
   your notes.
3. Continue north to **Chantilly Forest**. Read its study marker at (13,20),
   standing at (13,21) and facing north. These two markers may be read in
   either order, but both must be read **after accepting** Celine's survey.
4. Continue north into **Chantilly**. Speak to **Remy**, west of the guide,
   from (10,14), facing south. He reviews both observations.
5. Return to **Paris** and speak to Celine. Collect the **Miracle Seed**.
   The reward completes the survey and unlocks the second Gym.
6. Return to **Chantilly**, heal and enter the Gym. From the indoor arrival
   tile (8,18), follow this exact path to Marine:

   **Up 2, right 1, up 3, left 5, up 3, right 8, up 3, left 4.**

7. At (8,7), face north and speak to **Marine**. Accept the battle and win.

Marine uses **Psyduck level 12** and **Horsea level 14**. Grass or Electric
attacks help. Heal and train beforehand if the team is struggling.

**Completion check:** the Trainer Card now shows the **Cascadebadge** as well
as the first badge. Collect **TM03 Water Pulse**, or return for a pending TM
after making room. Save. To leave from Marine's approach tile, walk **right 4,
down 3, left 8, down 3, right 5, down 3, left 1, down 4**.

## Complete the German delivery and earn the third badge

1. Take the train to **Berlin**. Speak to **Lena**, west of the city guide,
   from (10,14), facing south. Both earlier badges are required. Choose YES
   to accept her repair parcel delivery.
2. Travel to **Oranienburg** and speak to **Karl**, west of the town guide,
   from (10,14), facing south. He takes the parts and gives you his report.
   The mission parcel and report are tracked by the quest, outside the Bag.
3. Return to **Berlin** and speak to Lena. Collect the **Magnet** to complete
   the delivery and unlock the third Gym.
4. Return to **Oranienburg**. Buy replacement Potions if needed and heal at
   the clinic. Enter the Gym. From (6,14), walk north eight tiles to (6,6),
   face north and speak to **Conrad**. Accept and win.

Conrad uses **Voltorb level 14** and **Pikachu level 16**. Avoid relying only
on a Water or Flying partner. Ground attacks are useful against this team;
a Grass partner resists Electric attacks. Bring Paralyze Heals and Potions.

For a Mudkip start, train it to level 16 and let it evolve into Marshtomp
before this battle. From Oranienburg, walk south on the central path to
Havel Trail, then train in the grass around (11,10). Buy ten Potions before
training. Return to the free Oranienburg clinic every six encounters to
restore HP, cure status and refill move PP. Heal after evolution, then use
Mud-Slap against Conrad. This preparation
was checked in the fresh Germany run; level-14 Mudkip alone lost this fight.

**Completion check:** you have the **Thunderbadge**, the third implemented
badge, and **TM34 Shock Wave** or its pending reward. Save. There is no fourth
Gym or Elite Four step in this prototype's completion route.

## Meet Ada and investigate Celebi

1. Travel to **Oxford**. Speak to **Ada** at (18,15), standing at (18,14) and
   facing south. With the Thunderbadge earned, accept her investigation.
2. Take the train to **Chantilly**. Follow the central path south into
   **Chantilly Forest**. Stand at (15,24), face west and speak to **Celebi**
   at (14,24), south of the forest survey marker.
3. Choose YES to stay still and listen. Advance through Celebi's picture
   and the vision of 1940. This is a conversation, not a capture battle.
4. Return to **Ada in Oxford** and report. Collect her **Rare Candy**. If the
   Items pocket is full, make room and report again.
5. Return to Celebi at the same forest position. Choose YES to travel into
   the past. You arrive in the **Chantilly station refuge in 1940**.

Celebi waits near each historical arrival point and can return you to the
present-day forest. Returning preserves historical quest progress. During the
early chapters, speak to present-day Celebi again to return to the refuge,
then use the guides and services to reach the place where you left off.

## Help Elise and verify the departure news

1. At the refuge, speak to **Elise** at (8,6), approaching from (8,7) and
   facing north. Finish her conversation about her cold Eevee.
2. Speak to the **keeper** at (5,6), approaching from (5,7), facing north.
   Collect the blanket.
3. Speak to **Elise again** to deliver it. Eevee settles into the blanket.
4. Speak to the **guide** at (12,8), approaching from (12,9), facing north.
   Choose YES to visit the station post.
5. At the post, read the **northeast noticeboard** at (11,4), approaching
   from (11,5), facing north.
6. Speak to the **dispatcher** at (8,6), approaching from (8,7), facing
   north. He confirms that a departure time is not yet known.
7. Speak to the **return guide** at (5,6), approaching from (5,7), facing
   north. Choose YES to return to the refuge.
8. Report to the **keeper**. This hand-in completes the departure-news task.
9. Use the southeast guide to visit the post again. Read the **same northeast
   board**. It now offers the train to **Beauvais**; choose YES to board.

## Check in at Beauvais and carry Elise's message

1. At **Beauvais reception**, speak to the **host** at (4,4), approaching
   from (4,5), facing north. He checks Elise and Eevee in together.
2. Speak to **Elise** at (7,6), approaching from (7,7), facing north. Advance
   through Eevee's picture and accept the request to carry her message.
3. Leave by the **south return service** to the Chantilly post. From the
   Beauvais arrival tile (4,7), walk down twice. At the post, use the return
   guide to visit the refuge.
4. Speak to the **refuge keeper** to deliver the message and receive her reply.
5. Return via the southeast guide to the post. Read the northeast board and
   board to **Beauvais** again.
6. Speak to **Elise** to deliver the keeper's reply.
7. Return to the present with Celebi beside the Beauvais arrival point. From
   (4,7), face west and press A, then choose YES.
8. Travel to **Oxford** and report to **Ada**. Collect the **Luxury Ball**.
   This archived first account is required for the next Beauvais tasks.

## Supply Beauvais and reunite Luc with Pidgey

1. Return to Celebi in present-day Chantilly Forest. Travel to the past,
   use the refuge guide to reach the post and board to **Beauvais**.
2. Speak to the **host** and accept the care-supply request with YES.
3. Take the south return service to the post. Speak to the **dispatcher**
   to collect the sealed parcel.
4. Read the northeast board to board to **Beauvais**. Speak to the host
   again to deliver the parcel. His free party-care service is now available.
5. Speak to **Elise** and choose YES to visit the **reception garden**.
6. Speak to **Luc** on the west side, at (6,8), from (6,9), facing north.
   Accept his request and learn the tune.
7. Approach **Pidgey** by the northeast trees. Stand at (17,6), face north
   and press A. You whistle the tune; Pidgey follows you.
8. Return to **Luc** and speak again to reunite them.
9. Use the garden's south guide to return to Beauvais reception, then its
   south return service to the Chantilly post.
10. Speak to the **dispatcher**. Choose YES to board the onward train to
    **Amiens**. This offer comes from the dispatcher, not the Beauvais board.

## Complete Amiens reception and the Meowth reunion

1. Speak to **Nora** on the west path at (6,8), approaching from (6,9),
   facing north. She asks you to read the reception instructions.
2. Read the **northeast noticeboard** at (17,5), approaching from (17,6),
   facing north.
3. Return to **Nora** and confirm the instructions.
4. Speak to **Nora again** and accept the request to find Mira's Meowth.
5. Speak to **Mira** on the north path at (13,8), from (13,9), facing north.
6. Speak to the **porter** on the east side at (18,12), from (18,13), facing
   north. Mira and the porter may be visited in either order after accepting.
7. Return to **Nora** to verify the matching descriptions.
8. Speak to **Mira again**. Meowth moves to her side, completing the reunion.
   Nora can now heal your party for free.
9. Use Celebi near the Amiens arrival point to return to the present. Travel
   to **Oxford** and speak to **Ada**. Choose YES to record the Amiens account.
   This particular account is required to unlock the next bulletin task.
10. Return through Celebi to the refuge, use its guide to reach the post and
    ask the dispatcher to board to **Amiens** again.

## Verify the Amiens bulletin and recover the Rouen book

1. Speak to the **Amiens porter** and accept the new bulletin task.
2. Read the **northeast noticeboard** again.
3. Return to the **porter** to have the exact instructions checked.
4. Report the checked news to **Nora**. She now has the waiting plan.
5. Speak to the **porter again**. Choose YES for the confirmed service to
   **Rouen**.
6. At Rouen, speak to **Leon** on the west path at (6,8), from (6,9), facing
   north, to register your arrival.
7. Speak to **Leon again** and accept his missing route-book request.
8. From the Rouen arrival tile (10,16), take **right 15, up 6, right 8,
   up 4**. You stand at (33,6), across the paved crossing. Face west and
   press A to collect the wrapped book at (32,6).
9. Return along the bank and paved crossing to **Leon**. Speak to return the
   book. He now offers free party care.
10. Speak to the **onward clerk** at (18,14), approaching from (18,15), facing
    north. Choose YES to travel to **Le Havre**.

## Clear the Le Havre dock and cross to Southampton

1. At the Le Havre arrival tile (8,5), face south and speak to the **captain**
   at (8,6). He records your arrival and offers a return to Rouen. Choose
   **NO** to stay at Le Havre.
2. Speak to the **dockworker** at (10,3). Approach from (9,3), face east and
   accept his request to check the closed-door notice.
3. Read the **notice** at (8,2), standing at (8,3), facing north.
4. Return to the **captain** from (8,5), facing south. He confirms the dock
   instructions. Decline the return to Rouen to remain here.
5. Speak to the **crossing clerk** at (5,3), approaching from (6,3), facing
   west. Choose YES to travel to **Southampton**.

Dock clearance unlocks the crossing immediately. You do **not** have to go
back to Oxford to file the port account first. The dockworker now offers free
party care.

## Recover the Southampton luggage and finish in London

1. At the Southampton arrival tile (8,5), face south and speak to the **host**
   at (8,6). He records your arrival. Choose **NO** when offered the return
   to Le Havre.
2. Speak to the **worker** at (10,3), approaching from (9,3), facing east.
   Accept the request to find the bag with a green label.
3. Walk to (6,3), face west and press A to collect the bag at (5,3). It only
   appears after accepting the search.
4. Return to the **worker** and speak to hand it in. He now offers free care.
5. Speak to the **London transport clerk** at (10,4), approaching from (9,4),
   facing east. Choose YES to travel to **London reception in 1940**.
   The Southampton account with Ada is not required to board.
6. Speak to **Rose** on the west path at (6,8), approaching from (6,9), facing
   north. Finish her welcome. This completes the historical London chapter.
7. Return to the arrival tile (10,16). Face west and speak to **Celebi**;
   choose YES to return to the present-day Chantilly Forest.
8. Travel to **Oxford** and speak to **Ada** at (18,15). Read her concluding
   account. She says, **"Your account is complete."**
9. If you deferred the port or Southampton accounts, Ada may offer them
   after her conclusion. Choose YES to file each account, speaking again
   as needed. These reports can still be filed after Rose's welcome.
10. Use the normal SAVE command. You can Continue and explore after the ending.

**Main story complete:** all three badges earned, Elise and Eevee sheltered,
Luc and Pidgey reunited, Mira and Meowth reunited, Rouen's route book returned,
dock instructions confirmed, Southampton luggage returned, Rose's welcome
finished and Ada's conclusion read. This prototype uses these conversations
as its ending; do not look for an Elite Four or a credits trigger.

## Optional activities and additional completion goals

These activities do not gate Rose's welcome or Ada's main conclusion.

### Complete all three landmark cases

| City | Case | Entrance approach | Reward |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paris | The Quiet Bells at Notre Dame | (28,35), face north | Spell Tag |
| London | A Place to Rest at Westminster | (14,35), face north | Rare Candy |
| Berlin | The Broken Signal at the Reichstag | (21,33), face north | Three Great Balls |

For each case, press A at the entrance and choose YES. Inside, speak to the
curator from (8,15), facing north, and accept the investigation. Read both
side records, from (15,8) and (4,6), facing north. Then speak to the attendant
from (10,5), facing north, and choose YES for the peaceful resolution. Return
to the curator for the reward. Read the case ledger from (12,14), facing south.
After all three rewards have been collected, the ledger and Ada can give the
combined case synthesis. The post-case Gastly battle at Notre Dame is optional.

### Optional Gastly capture at Notre Dame

After collecting the **Quiet Bells** reward, return to the attendant from
(10,5), facing north. Choose YES to encounter a **level-12 Gastly**, or NO/B
to leave the encounter for later. The case remains completed either way.

Make sure you have room in your party and bring capture balls. The three
Great Balls awarded by the Reichstag case can be used here. Station shops
also sell Poke Balls for 200 each; ten cost 2000. In battle, select **BAG**,
switch to **POKE BALLS**, choose a ball and select USE. If Gastly breaks out,
throw another ball. You can attempt capture without attacking; the verified
run caught it at full HP using two earned Great Balls. Your result may take
more throws, so bring spare balls and healing supplies.

After capture, finish the Pokedex/nickname prompts. Gastly joins your party
when there is a free slot. Save/Continue retains it, and the main ending and
all completed cases remain available. Catching Gastly is optional and does
not gate any story step or the three-case synthesis.

### Collect tour stamps and explore

Speak to the guide in each of **London, Paris and Berlin**, approaching
from (16,14) and facing south. Collect the three stamps in any order. The
third guide awards one **Exp. Share** if the Items pocket has room. If it is
full, your stamps remain recorded: make room and speak to any of these three
guides again. Repeat talks do not grant another Exp. Share. You can complete
this tour after the main ending and landmark cases; Save/Continue keeps it.
Additional route trainers, captures, the Bicycle, ferries, coastal
coaches, visitor galleries, homes, reading rooms and garden workrooms remain
available. Use the map's Places and Rooms pages to find them. Present-day
Dover/Calais crossings are separate from the historical Le Havre/Southampton
story crossing.

Approach the door from the tile listed below, face north, press A and

| Paris | Western sketch room, red roof | (45,39) | Display cabinet: (9,2), face north |

Inside any of these three rooms, speak to the host from (8,5), facing

All three visits and both exit tiles were checked after the main ending,

capital, press R for Places, then L for Rooms. This checked route covers
the three western homes above, rather than every visitor interior.

### Use the Bicycle

The Bicycle is already in your starting Bag; you do not need to earn or
buy it. Open START, BAG, KEY ITEMS, select BICYCLE and choose USE. Use the
D-pad to ride. To get off, use BICYCLE from KEY ITEMS again.

Normal Save/Continue keeps you riding at the saved outdoor position.
Mounting, movement, riding Continue and dismounting were checked in Oxford
after story completion, optional cases, stamps and Gastly capture. Your
party, items and completed quests remain intact. Riverboat and rental
Lapras boarding while riding are covered in their separate steps below.

### London/Oxford riverboat

The riverboat is optional, free and requires the **Thunderbadge**.

1. In either **London** or **Oxford**, stand at (24,16), face south and
   speak to the riverboat captain. From the town-guide approach at (16,14),
   walk east eight tiles and south two tiles to reach this boarding point.
2. Choose YES to sail directly to the other town. NO or B leaves you at
   your current landing. You arrive at (24,16) in the destination town.
3. Save/Continue retains that landing. The Town Map shows London or Oxford
   in the present day. Speak south to the captain again for the return trip.
4. To reach the town square, walk north two tiles and west eight tiles,
   returning to (16,14). Boarding while using the Bicycle is also supported;
   you can still move normally after landing.

Both directions have been checked after story, case and stamp completion.
The trips leave those rewards and progress intact.

### Borrow Lapras at London or Oxford

This optional rental is free with the **Thunderbadge**. You do not need an
HM or a free party slot; the rental does not add Lapras to your team.

1. Stand at (26,16), face south and speak to the rental attendant. This is
   two tiles east of the riverboat captain's boarding approach.
2. Advance the introduction, press A to close the Lapras picture, then
   choose YES. NO or B at the rental question leaves you on land.
3. You board at (26,19). Use the direction buttons to steer. A checked
   practice route is west two tiles to (24,19), then east two tiles back.
   Saving at (26,19) and using Continue retains the ride and steering.
4. From (26,19), move west one tile, then north two tiles to the clear bank
   at (25,17). You dismount automatically and can walk normally.

These steps were checked at both landings after the main ending, cases and

### Present-day Dover/Calais coaches and ferry

These optional trips require the **Thunderbadge** and are free. They are
separate from the historical Le Havre/Southampton story crossing.

1. Enter the station in **London** or **Paris** through the door at (23,9),
   approached from (23,10). Inside, stand at (4,5), face north and speak to
   the coach attendant. Choose YES for **Dover** from London or **Calais**
   from Paris. NO or B leaves you at the station.
2. You arrive at the port at (8,5). Face south and speak to the captain.
   Choose YES to sail to the other present-day port. NO or B leaves you
   at your current port.
3. After either arrival, Save/Continue keeps you at that port. The Town Map
   identifies Dover or Calais in the present day.
4. To return to a capital, start at the port arrival point (8,5) and walk
   north three tiles. Dover returns you to London's station entrance at
   (23,10); Calais returns you to Paris's station entrance at (23,10).

Both directions have been checked after the main ending, cases and stamp
tour. These trips do not consume money or reset those completed activities.
