# European Tour completion checklist

This is the single release checklist. Versioned ROADMAP entries are history, not an open task queue.

## Defined finished game

Release v3.0 completes the existing European Tour: all three country studies and Gyms, Celebi's care-and-reunion journey through historical London, Rose's welcome, and Ada's final Oxford conclusion. The ending explicitly says MAIN STORY COMPLETE. Save/Continue and free exploration remain available afterward. Optional stamps, landmark cases and delayed accounts remain playable and do not block the ending. No Elite Four is required by this scope.

Additional countries, a longer wartime sequel and USA player transfer belong to a later expansion. Existing town music, animated Celebi title, avatar/outfit options, bicycle, Lapras rental and visitor rooms remain included.
