# Eclipse postgame roadmap

The ending keeps League shelters, country travel and repeatable training available.

- [x] Eclipse-remnant hunt in Scandinavia: investigate surviving relays across
  Denmark, Norway and Sweden, battle remnant commanders, and shut down their
  final shadow signal. Connect the investigation to the northern guardians.
- [x] Sanctuary restoration in Krakow: add a distinct Krakow district alongside
  Warsaw, rebuild its rescue sanctuary, and let earlier timeline choices affect
  restoration tasks and the partners returning to the refuge.
- [x] Champion rematch circuit: add stronger teams, complete movesets and held
  items for the eight leaders, League council and rival champion; preserve
  difficulty settings and award a saved circuit completion reward.
- [x] Pre-League legendary encounters: Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres.
- [x] Postgame legendary encounters: Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh and Mewtwo.
- [ ] Finish natural-level campaign balancing and broad hardware/emulator checks.
  One Normal-mode, non-Nuzlocke saved-party route now reaches all eight badges,
  the wartime chapters, Veil, all four council members and the champion ending,
  with six partners and three purifications. Natural captures now
  cover Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres before the League, plus Suicune, Lugia and
  Ho-Oh and Mewtwo after it, including cold-saved PC delivery. All six rescues
  were purified using native PC deposits, care and country travel. Timer Balls
  are available in League shops for long battles. Continue with Mewtwo balancing,
  rematch combat, other starting routes and difficulty balancing. The earned
  champion party completed the Scandinavia hunt and cold-saved its unique
  reward; Boreal required 13 Hyper Potions and one Revive on this route. The
  same save then earned the Warsaw garden decision, restored Krakow using
  purchased materials and cold-saved the Lucky Egg and boxed Eevee adoption.
  Past-map and player palettes survive cold Continue and the modern return.
