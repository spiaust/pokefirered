# Eclipse postgame roadmap

The ending keeps League shelters, country travel and repeatable training available.

- [x] Eclipse-remnant hunt in Scandinavia: investigate surviving relays across
  Denmark, Norway and Sweden, battle remnant commanders, and shut down their
  final shadow signal. Connect the investigation to the northern guardians.
- [x] Sanctuary restoration in Krakow: add a distinct Krakow district alongside
  Warsaw, rebuild its rescue sanctuary, and let earlier timeline choices affect
  restoration tasks and the partners returning to the refuge.
- [ ] Champion rematch circuit: add stronger teams, complete movesets and held
  items for the eight leaders, League council and rival champion; preserve
  difficulty settings and award a saved circuit completion reward.
- [x] Pre-League legendary encounters: Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres.
- [x] Postgame legendary encounters: Suicune, Lugia, Ho-Oh and Mewtwo.
- [ ] Finish natural-level campaign balancing and broad hardware/emulator checks.
